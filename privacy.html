Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FlamingoHelper (the “Bot”) handles information when used on Discord.

1. Information processed

To operate its features, the Bot may process and store Discord identifiers and server configuration, such as server (guild), channel, role, and user IDs, together with settings created by server administrators and information needed to execute Bot commands. The exact information depends on which features are enabled and used.

2. Database and storage

The Bot uses MongoDB to store the data required for its operation. Data is accessible only to the Bot operator and authorized people who need it to maintain the service.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide the Bot’s features, remember server settings, respond to commands, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability. It is not sold to third parties.

4. Sharing

Information may be processed by Discord as part of the Discord platform and by the hosting or database providers used to operate the Bot. It may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for as long as it is needed to operate the Bot or meet legitimate operational and legal requirements. Server administrators may request deletion of stored server data by contacting the Bot developer. Some data may remain in backups for a limited period.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children and eligibility

The Bot is a Discord application and must be used in accordance with Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable law.

8. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the Bot’s data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when updates are made.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or a data deletion request, contact the Bot developer through the support contact or server listed on the Bot’s Discord profile.
